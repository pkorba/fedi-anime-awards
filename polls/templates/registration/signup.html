Frequently Asked Questions

How does this registration form work?

  1. Enter a Lemmy/Piefed/Mbin username of an account that you control. For example: kurisu@ani.social. Remember that this field is case-sensitive!
  2. Answer the control question.
  3. Agree to Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  4. Hit Register button.
  5. Check your inbox on your Lemmy/Piefed/Mbin account. You will receive an automatically generated password there, sent to you by a bot. On desktop, you can open your inbox by clicking the bell button in the top-right corner of the page.
  6. Use the username you entered and the password you received to log in on the main page.
  7. That's it! After that, you should be able to vote.

I did not receive the private message with my password!

  1. Your instance might be defederated from ani.social. Lemmy.ml is one of such instances. In this case you won't receive the password, because your instance ignores all private messages from users registered on ani.social (like our bot).
    To check if your instance is defederated from ani.social, on your instance navigate to the footer (bottom of the page), click Instances, and view the Blocked instances tab.
  2. If you use Piefed or Mbin, check your account settings. There is an option there that allows you to block messages from outside your own instance. On Piefed it's:
    Settings / Preferences / Accept private messages from
    For the time of registration, set it to All instances.
    On Mbin it's:
    Settings / General / Privacy / Who can send you a direct message. Similarly, set it to Everyone for the time of registration.
    If you already tried signing up, and it looked like the registration succeeded, but you didn't receive the password because of this setting, your account probably has been successfully created, but your instance silently ignored the message containing your password. We're not able to send you your password again. In order to regain access to your account, reset your password.
