Voting instructions

  1. During the nominations, you may select up to 5 items in each category (you can choose fewer, or none at all), whereas in the final round, you may select only one item per category.
  2. There is no button in the form to save your choices. All your votes are saved automatically the moment you select or deselect an item.
  3. On every page there's a search bar which allows you to filter visible entries. On the Character and Voice Performance pages, you must enter a search term to display any items, as there are too many to show the entire list at once.
  4. If an error occurs during the saving process, you will be notified via a ❌ icon in the area that lists your currently selected items.
  5. Icon 🔄 means that changes are awaiting to be saved. Don't close the browser tab in this state, because you might lose your changes. Navigating to a different category or home page is fine.
  6. Icon ✅ means that changes were successfully saved.
  7. Selected items are moved to the top of the list.
  8. All your choices will be displayed in a summary at the end of the voting process.
  9. You can review your choices at any time by clicking the Show my votes button above.
  10. You may change your choices at any time, as long as the voting remains open. Clicking START button does not clear any of your previous choices.
  11. On the page listing all your votes, there is a button that allows you to copy your votes as Markdown-formatted text. This is a small convenience in case you’d like to share your votes on a forum or on AniList. It has no impact on the validity of your ballot.
